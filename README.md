# Toystack Stage 1 Pilot

A one-page plan by Alisha Chaudhari for the **Product Manager / Client Implementation Partner** take-home at Toystack AI: how to run a six-week pilot of a drug-discovery literature search.

**Live page:** https://alisha-19.github.io/toystack-stage1-pilot/

## What's inside
- **Task 1, plan of action:** the scope (type in a protein, get a shortlist of molecules with linked evidence), the data sources (licensed and free only), how the pieces connect, the first step, and the assumptions, risks and open questions.
- **Task 2, six-week delivery plan:** milestones and team, dependencies and risks, tracking, success and acceptance criteria, and a clear cut list.
- **Task 3, email to the client's VP of Research:** a short, plain-language update.
- **Task 4, three weeks in:** what to do in the next 24 hours, how to work with engineering, the options to put to the client, and how to protect the date without losing trust.
- **How I used AI**, plus references.

## Key decisions
- **Evidence, not verdicts:** every molecule links to the exact sentence or table it came from. The scientist decides, not an AI score.
- **Licensed sources only:** ChEMBL, UniProt, Europe PMC, PMC Open Access, PubChem, PDB/AlphaFold. A lawyer confirms the licences in week 1.
- **One protein, end to end:** working by week 2, full papers by week 3, client testing in week 5, demo in week 6.

Built from the take-home brief and public source documentation. Data licences were checked on 3 November 2026 and should be confirmed again before anyone relies on them.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, no build step.
